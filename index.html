What we make

Three product lines, one standard.

Every formula starts with gentle, plant-based ingredients and is produced in our own facility from mixing to packing.

Natural bar soaps

Cold-process and glycerin bars made with olive, coconut and shea oils.

  • Unscented & essential-oil scents
  • Goat milk, oat, clay & charcoal variants
  • 100 g and 150 g formats

Liquid soaps

Hand and body washes with mild, skin-friendly surfactants.

  • 1 L and 5 L refill containers

Hotel & guest amenities
